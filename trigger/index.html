Aerobotix · hand-tracked AR weapon

TRIGGER

  1. DRAWMake a finger gun: index finger out, thumb up, other fingers curled. The rifle appears in your hand.
  2. AIMPoint across the frame, left or right. The barrel follows your finger.
  3. FIREDrop your thumb onto your index finger. Hold it down for full auto.
  4. RELOADAutomatic when the 30-round mag is empty. Open your hand to holster.
Use both hands to dual-wield. Turn the volume up — the shots are loud. Tap or hold the screen as a backup trigger.
