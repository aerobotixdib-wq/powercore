HOLO RIFLE

  1. SUMMONTouch the floating rifle icon at the top with your index finger in the air. The hologram builds in your hands.
  2. HOLDTwo hands like a real rifle: trigger hand on the grip, other hand forward under the barrel.
  3. AIMMove both hands. The rifle follows the line between them in 3D. The crosshair shows where it hits.
  4. FIRESqueeze the index finger of your trigger hand. Hold it curled for full auto.
